# Contributing

Identify the edition, section and observable claim when reporting a correction through this repository's Issues or Pull requests. A useful contribution supplies a small synthetic fixture and an expected output that another reader can check.

Examples include a request copied from a hidden instruction, a paid order lacking an access grant, a purchase offset by a displaced purchase, or two simulated workers whose power-circuit labels differ while their supply is shared. Include the ordinary success case, the failure case, and the reason the scorer should distinguish them. For playback studies, document the rights to every audio fixture.

For an executed study report the software versions, independent units, frozen budget and scoring rule. Preserve refused jobs, failed runs and missing observations. The [research record](agent-manifest.json) lists the designs and their execution status. [Reuse terms](RIGHTS.md) apply to the original text and metadata.
