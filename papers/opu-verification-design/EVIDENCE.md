# Evidence Scope and Reproduction Limits

This is a retrospective local audit and conceptual research program. It is not a randomized organizational intervention or a demonstration of autonomous evolution.

| Case | Available basis | Publicly reportable conclusion | Remaining gap |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Classification | Two saved structured outputs and bounded original discussion; offline alignment | Eight items, two primary-label changes, five any-field changes, sixteen certainty markers | No gold labels, repeated same-order control or isolated ordering cause |
| Speech recognition | Attributed secondary audit summary | Parameter modification and correction of a known target plus three known regression passages are reported | Original training/checkpoint evidence and unseen-audio evaluation not independently verified |
| Harness | Early secondary summary and a distinct later saved report | Negative comparison reported; later four phase-level runs produced no delivery artifacts | Earlier paired total-cost evidence incomplete; later resource interference and pending interface review remain |
| Resource governance | Direct artifacts and ten saved file-hash comparisons | Registry, rules and audit configuration implemented; artifact integrity checked | Runtime acceptance, cross-station adoption, later audit execution and net benefits not established |

Raw conversations, task passages, original audio, personal information, asset inventories, billing details and operational locators are not public. The package cannot independently reproduce the private retrospective cases. It publishes their limited summaries without presenting them as an open empirical dataset.

The prospective protocol uses fresh synthetic data and sealed evaluation. It has not been run. No new inference, training or model acquisition was performed for this paper's retrospective audit. A saved-result check is not a second model run, and a publication is not adoption.

The reference inventory distinguishes accessible primary material from metadata/indexed-abstract-only verification. It makes no claim to a systematic literature review or inaccessible full-text reading.
