# Human Verification Design and Multi-Agent Organizational Learning

A research paper on a One Person University: how a person can design feedback, independent evaluation and bounded update authority without supplying every task answer.

## Main result

The evidence supports local observations and implemented governance artifacts, not autonomous organizational evolution. Two saved classification passes cover eight items: two primary labels changed, five items changed in at least one classification field, and all sixteen item outputs asserted certainty. These are instability observations, not accuracy estimates or causal ordering effects.

Other cases distinguish a reported targeted speech-recognition repair from unseen-input generalization, preserve negative Harness outcomes, and separate resource-registry implementation from actual adoption and net benefits.
