# Contributions

Useful contributions include a specific factual correction with a primary source, a counterexample to a stated verification relation, a clearer independence argument, or a preregistered synthetic experiment testing the proposed mechanism against a strong baseline.

Distinguish proposals, saved-result recalculations, new executions and independent replications. Report negative and mixed results, complete cost boundaries and unknown quantities. Do not treat role labels as independent evaluation or consistency as correctness.

Do not submit private conversations, credentials, personal data, proprietary task records or unauthorized media. Share original synthetic fixtures or data with explicit rights. Follow the hosting project's contribution policies and avoid duplicate or non-incremental submissions.

Changes intended for this paper are contributed under CC0 1.0 unless a different arrangement is explicitly agreed before inclusion. Cited and referenced third-party materials retain their existing rights. This document does not authorize external actions or spending.
