# Evidence and interpretation

This is a conceptual manuscript and prospective design. No new model run, training, economic transaction, educator trial, or measured parameter-efficiency result is reported in version 0.1.0.

The source list records exact references and the access scope of each check. Several works were checked at abstract or selected-section level, not read as a full systematic review. Their experiments were not independently reproduced. Niche construction is used as a limited biological analogy.

The manuscript distinguishes demand articulation, purchasing power, exchange and net additional demand; memory, configuration and numerical weight updates; competitive pressure and a learning mechanism; observable preferences and subjective experience.

The first design uses human-authored tool modules. It cannot establish peer-created supply, autonomous teaching, neural-weight evolution, collective aesthetic culture, or a self-sustaining economy. Its four world trajectories are feasibility units, not a powered population study. Counts of tasks or rounds must not be reported as independent agents.

The initial budget and progression criteria are prospective. Exact stimuli, implementation, independent scoring, resource authorization and runtime settings must be sealed before execution. Unperformed outcomes are unknown rather than zero. A gate failure is a feasibility finding and must not be relabeled proof that agents lack demand.

Future educator research must measure learner transfer, total teacher and learner cost, solvability, leakage, and retention after leaving the teacher. Difficulty, sales and adoption alone are insufficient evidence of teaching value.
