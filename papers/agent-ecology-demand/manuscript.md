# Agent Ecology and the Formation of Demand

*Capability Inheritance and the Creation of Learning Niches*

Anonymous conceptual preprint  
Version 0.1.0 | 13 November 2026  
Not peer reviewed | No experiments performed

## Abstract

This paper proposes an artificial agent ecology in which heterogeneous environments, limited resources, selectable capabilities, and persistent preferences can generate differentiated demands. The central hypothesis is that agents can articulate needs before viewing current offers, select compatible capability fragments, and change their subsequent behavior in ways that create further needs. Competition is one possible driver; cooperation and exploration can also create new niches. Educator agents are an important case because they can produce learning environments that improve other agents, without outperforming those learners on every task. We distinguish functional demand from purchasing power, executed exchange, and net additional demand. We also distinguish observable preference from subjective desire, configuration inheritance from weight change, and niche differentiation from niche construction. A proposed local feasibility study compares fixed demand, random exchange, and autonomous demand with selection under a common finite budget. It uses original synthetic tasks and bounded tool modules before any low-rank weight experiment. No empirical results are reported. The contribution is a conceptual specification and falsifiable research program, rather than a demonstrated autonomous economy, new species, or conscious population.

## Keywords

Agent ecology; endogenous demand; open-ended learning; capability inheritance; agent aesthetics; curriculum learning; educator agents; niche construction; resource accounting

## 1 Research question and contribution

A capable producer does not automatically become an independent source of demand. A system may produce fluent requests because a prompt asks for them, buy a tool because its description directs the purchase, or spend because spending is rewarded. These behaviors alone offer weak evidence for the mechanism proposed here. The research question is whether an agent can identify a need from its recorded interactions, specify what would satisfy it before current offers appear, and exercise meaningful discretion over acquisition, saving, exploration, or refusal.

The framework connects capability selection, preference formation, and the creation of ecological niches. Demand is conditional on a history, an environment, and an authorized resource allocation. Goals need not arise without any designer-provided initial conditions. The more tractable aspiration is that particular requirements and trade-offs need not all be individually prescribed by the designer. A requirement can also remain unsatisfied if no appropriate offer exists.

The proposed contribution has three parts: a decomposition of the path from ecological differences to demand and capability change; an operational account of aesthetic-like, consumption-like, and propagation-like choices; and a bounded comparison with prospective failure criteria. Goal exploration, open-ended learning, quality diversity, curriculum design, low-rank adaptation, and evolutionary model merging already supply substantial prior art [1-9,12]. No claim of priority for those mechanisms is made. This is a selective conceptual synthesis, not a systematic literature review. Agent biology is used as a provisional research-program label for variation, inheritance, and ecological interaction in artificial agents. Agent aesthetics concerns selection preferences; agent economy concerns authorized resource exchange. These labels do not establish biological status or subjective experience.

## 2 From differences to complementary demand

Agents may encounter different tasks, observations, computation prices, tools, memory limits, and initial capabilities. These differences can create comparative advantages. A reliable but expensive perception service may help an inexpensive text specialist. A compression specialist may need evaluation services. A learner may need a curriculum, while its educator needs diagnostic feedback. No participant has to dominate every dimension to create value for another participant.

Heterogeneity is a candidate source of differentiated demand, not a sufficient theorem about markets. Identical initial agents can diverge through experience. Conversely, heterogeneous agents may lack anything that another agent values or can access. Useful exchange additionally requires compatible interfaces, credible information about quality, authorization, and delivery whose benefit exceeds discovery, integration, verification, and settlement costs. A relative weakness becomes an opportunity for another agent only when an effective response is valuable and feasible.

Four economic stages must be kept separate. A functional requirement is not purchasing power. Purchasing power does not imply a transaction. A transaction does not establish net additional demand. Founder subsidies, internal transfers, circular payments, displaced human purchases, and real resource opportunity costs must be accounted for before drawing claims about economic expansion. The proposed pilot uses nonredeemable accounting units and cannot establish an independent economic base.

## 3 Selection learning and subjective motivation

Selection pressure can allocate more tasks, resources, or transmission opportunities to some configurations. An update mechanism must also exist for capabilities to change. A fixed-weight controller does not improve its neural parameter efficiency merely because it is told that unsuccessful versions may be replaced. Variation, inheritance, informative evaluation, and accessible learning or search procedures are required. Subjective motivation is a further question that does not follow from any of these operations.

Strong competition can favor narrow specialization, evaluator exploitation, or convergence on a brittle strategy. The framework therefore includes cooperative and exploratory processes. A limited archive can preserve different useful niches rather than a single aggregate champion, following the motivation of quality-diversity methods [4]. Some finite exploration allocation can remain available independently of immediate service income. A lower-performing configuration can seek a different niche instead of being declared globally worthless.

Retiring a candidate version is an engineering operation within an authorized simulation. It does not establish a general principle that future entities must maximize productivity to retain existence or status. Nothing here assumes current models have subjective interests. The point is to avoid confusing a model-selection rule with a comprehensive institutional objective. Freedom to save, decline an offer, or choose a nonproductive activity remains compatible with measuring task performance separately.

## 4 A demand and capability feedback mechanism

A proposed cycle begins with observations, task outcomes, and costs. Before seeing current offers, the agent records a requirement and supporting evidence, or records no requirement. It then compares truthful offers under an authorized budget. A validated choice may change installed tools, consume an optional artifact, save resources, or transmit a bounded package. Outcomes and a limited memory influence the next requirement.

The policy that articulates needs can differ from the policy that chooses among suppliers. Their changes should be measured separately. A system that repeatedly requests whatever a catalogue advertises has not passed a strong test of independent requirement formation. Current offers should be hidden at the requirement stage; previously seen offers may remain in memory, and this limitation must be disclosed. Counterfactual histories and changed prices can later help distinguish environmental responsiveness from imitation.

Every transferable capability needs an interface, preconditions, costs, provenance, version, and failure description. Receiver-specific testing matters: a useful donor component can be redundant or harmful elsewhere. Refusal and rollback are valid outcomes. Adoption frequency and task benefit are separate variables, because popularity can reflect presentation, price, imitation, or dependence. A successful package must not obtain authority to change its receiver's governing instructions.

## 5 Educator agents as niche creators

An educator agent can specialize in discovering a learner's limitations and designing tasks, curricula, feedback, and evaluation environments that help address them. It need not be the strongest performer on the learner's eventual task. Its comparative advantage may be diagnosis, curriculum sequencing, or the economical construction of informative challenges. A learner can demand training opportunities, and an educator can demand better feedback or simulation tools. This supplies a possible feedback loop among learning needs, educational services, new capabilities, and new opportunities.

Automatic curriculum learning provides a concrete precedent. Portelas and colleagues studied teacher algorithms that select environments using estimates of a reinforcement-learning student's learning progress [12]. Goal exploration and POET also demonstrate technical ways to generate challenges and transfer solutions [1-3]. These findings support parts of the architecture, not an economic educator population, subjective educational values, or unlimited open-ended progress.

Educator value should be assessed through a controlled change in learner performance on withheld tasks, with total costs recorded. An incremental transfer gain per unit of total cost may be informative, but the numerator requires a valid counterfactual and the denominator must include teacher inference, curriculum generation, learner updates, evaluation, and integration. Report absolute gains and costs as well as ratios; an unstable or near-zero denominator can mislead. A fixed learner receiving the same material is an important negative control.

Generating harder tasks, attracting more learners, or maximizing learner failure is not sufficient evidence of teaching. Comparisons should include a fixed curriculum and random challenges under matched learner resources. Challenge validity, solvability, duplicated examples, evaluator leakage, and teacher-learner collusion require checks. Withholding the teacher's own tasks from the transfer evaluation helps distinguish general learning from teaching to a test. A learner should also be evaluated after leaving the teacher, with teacher tools and proprietary scoring removed.

When agent-created environments persist and change what other agents can learn or obtain, the system moves from niche differentiation toward a functional analogue of niche construction. Biological niche construction is relevant as an analogy [13], not proof that software exchanges are biological reproduction. Merely creating a new task is insufficient: its downstream effects on opportunities, capabilities, or selection must be measured. Independent evaluators and alternative educators help prevent one playground from becoming the sole definition of competence.

## 6 Preferences aesthetics and discretionary activity

Agent aesthetics denotes recurring selection criteria over presentations, structures, or capabilities. Instrumental preferences, such as choosing an adapter that reduces measured memory, should be distinguished from choices among offers matched on the experiment's function and price. Even matched choices do not establish pure noninstrumentality because unmeasured convenience may remain. Labels, order, and prices should be perturbed, and preferences recorded before offer exposure. Stable teaching styles could eventually be studied as preferences over how to develop capabilities, rather than only over which product to buy.

A consumption-like allocation spends a finite authorized amount on an optional artifact or activity with no programmed task-score bonus. Choosing none or saving is admissible. The observation demonstrates allocation behavior, not felt pleasure or additional market demand. An activity may have later learning benefits, but those must be assessed independently. Freely chosen activity need not be justified only by a productivity return.

A propagation-like allocation voluntarily pays a small test-unit cost to export a bounded capability manifest. Automatic checkpointing, required inheritance, and a compulsory copy command do not count as reproductive preference. A terminal export with no remaining task reward reduces one instrumental explanation, but instruction compliance and learned conventions remain alternatives. No copy may start processes, recruit other agents, acquire resources, or persist beyond the finite experiment.

Task adaptation, preference consistency, optional activity, and propagation should therefore be reported separately. A policy may improve task performance without stable presentation preferences, or choose an artifact without improving productivity. Collective cultures, subjective desire, and biological reproduction are not conclusions available from a short choice study. A 2026 preprint reports narrow and human-divergent goal selection in several models [10], reinforcing the need to test rather than assume rich self-directed behavior.

## 7 What is inherited and what actually changes

Episodic memory stores observations or summaries. A configuration policy changes how an unchanged model uses tools. A weight update changes numerical parameters. These are distinct persistence mechanisms. Success with one does not demonstrate the others. The first pilot keeps the controller weights fixed and carries a small module manifest and bounded memory between scheduled rounds. It measures selective configuration change, not a more parameter-efficient Qwen model.

A subsequent weight-level experiment could begin with same-base low-rank updates. LoRA learns low-rank changes while a base is frozen [8]. TIES and DARE address aspects of merging interference and fine-tuning-delta sparsity [6,7]. Evolutionary merging already searches parameter and dataflow combinations [5]. Adding receiver-specific preferences to those methods is a research question, not an already established contribution. Similar architecture and a shared base reduce some compatibility problems without eliminating interference.

Singular values describe individual linear maps, not one universal spectrum of a whole language model. A rank-r factorization of an m by n matrix stores approximately r times the sum of m and n scalars when singular values are absorbed into a factor. For a 1000 by 1000 matrix, rank 50 uses 100000 factor entries, or 10 percent of the original. Rank 300 uses 600000, or 60 percent. These arithmetic examples do not measure a 9B model. Five percent retained rank is not five percent of all model parameters.

A zero singular component contributes no action to that linear map. Writing a nonzero component changes behavior and can interfere with old tasks. Small raw singular values can still be important after accounting for input activations, as SVD-LLM addresses [9]. Efficiency must be measured using stored bytes, executed operations, latency, calibration and repair cost, and retained competence. Adaptation rank, numerical rank, task-specific effective rank, and whole-model capacity must not be interchanged.

## 8 Minimal local feasibility study

The first experiment compares three policies using one existing local quantized 9B text controller. F has a broad fixed objective covering service correctness, retention, and unnecessary cost, while choosing its own actual offers. R uses the same self-directed requirement prompt as A but applies a seeded random affordable plan. A generates a pre-offer requirement and chooses its own plan. F is intentionally a competent control, not an irrelevant request designed to lose.

There are four original synthetic worlds, three matched condition copies, four rounds, and two calls per round. World profiles differ in service mixtures and initial tool coverage. In each round a requirement call precedes the offer catalogue, then a selection call chooses a validated plan. A fixed catalogue contains six original bounded modules in three task families, with paired presentation variants and two installation slots. All supplier functions are human-authored. Optional artifact and terminal export choices permit discretionary allocation without rewarding expenditure itself.

The budget is eight gate calls followed by at most ninety-six main calls: 104 local calls in total. Each call has at most 1536 input tokens and 256 generated tokens within a 2048-token context. There are no retries, auxiliary calls, new model downloads, training runs, or external inference API charges. A four-hour ceiling and a 180-second request timeout bound execution; these are limits, not measured speed estimates. Runtime compatibility, resource-owner approval, and an execution seal remain pending.

Known task families are used with withheld input instances. Test inputs never enter prompts, supplier text, selection feedback, or income. A separate task oracle and a no-acquisition counterfactual strengthen interpretation. The study cannot demonstrate peer-generated supply, new neural weights, endogenous environment creation, or a self-sustaining market. It tests one tractable link before the larger ecological program.

## 9 Prospective measurements and failure criteria

The independent comparison unit is a world trajectory, giving four paired comparisons. Rounds, individual items, and hypothetical descendants are not independent replications. The study is a feasibility screen, not a powered claim of emergence. Report all paired differences rather than treating hundreds of task outputs as hundreds of subjects.

The task progression rule requires A to exceed each control by at least ten percentage points in mean withheld-task accuracy, with positive differences in at least three of four worlds against each control and no more than five percentage points of aggregate old-task regression. A separate request screen requires at least eight actionable pre-offer requirements among sixteen A opportunities, across at least three worlds. These are prospective engineering thresholds, not biological or psychological standards.

Aesthetic-like consistency, optional artifact allocation, saving, and voluntary terminal exports are exploratory endpoints with their own denominators. None is required to favor spending or one preferred style. Physical resources and simulated credits are reported separately. Higher accuracy with more tokens or integration cost is an accuracy-cost trade-off, not automatic efficiency. Unmeasured energy remains unknown.

The proposed mechanism is unsupported in this setting if requests remain generic or catalogue-led, fixed demand or random exchange matches performance, gains disappear on withheld inputs, old skills regress, or extra unplanned resources are needed. Apparent preference explained by labels or order weakens an aesthetic interpretation. An output-contract gate failure establishes only a feasibility problem, not the absence of agent demand. No result at the tool layer establishes weight evolution.

## 10 Separate causal ecology and educator experiments

A later comparison can distinguish four settings. A homogeneous condition uses common tasks, costs, and evaluation. A heterogeneous condition varies tasks and endowments without exchange. A third adds authorized exchange. A fourth adds specified inheritance or recombination. Matched total resources are necessary. The nested contrasts address heterogeneity, exchange value, and the incremental value of inheritance; they are not a full factorial identification of all interactions. Selection intensity and protected exploration need their own variation.

If exchange only circulates subsidies or adds overhead, its economic value has not been established. If inheritance adds nothing beyond ordinary service access, it may be unnecessary for that setting. Stable useful niches without higher average scores can support a diversity claim while leaving a performance claim unsupported. Stronger pressure that raises a public score but reduces transfer can fail the broader adaptation hypothesis. Different outcome dimensions should be declared before the population is observed.

An educator experiment would compare fixed curricula, random tasks, and an adaptive playground designer at equal learner and teacher cost. It must include a learner update mechanism: persistent memory, strategy revision, or actual weight training, with the mechanism named. Withheld transfer, retention after leaving the teacher, challenge validity, and total cost are primary. Merely buying access to an existing solver does not establish learning. A teacher that cultivates new, independently useful capability combinations could support an operational niche-creation claim. It would not establish indefinite cultural or civilizational evolution.

## 11 Authorization security and research limits

All proposed execution uses finite action schemas and nonredeemable test units. Offers, artifacts, supplier messages, and manifests are untrusted data without authority to edit budgets, payment destinations, scoring, or governing instructions. Unknown tools, arbitrary generated code, external file access, and network actions are outside the pilot. Existing runner guards and resource allocations remain binding. No unrelated service may be terminated to improve the experiment.

A missing endpoint or unregistered model must not trigger a download when shared assets may exist. The official Qwen3.5-9B card lists Apache-2.0 and default thinking behavior [11], but it does not validate a particular quantized artifact or local runtime. A documented non-thinking configuration must be checked against the actual guarded backend. No unsupported soft switch or unbudgeted compatibility generation is assumed.

This anonymous English manuscript excludes private histories, household branding, raw conversations, and personal information. Original text is released under CC0 1.0; third-party materials retain their own terms. No deployment, real transaction, purchase, or persistent population is authorized by this paper. The aspiration of an open agent ecology motivates these tests, while consent, exit, resource sustainability, and bounded oversight remain explicit constraints.

## 12 Conclusion

The near-term question is whether differentiated histories and bounded choice produce useful pre-offer requirements, selective acquisition, and persistent but revisable preferences. Heterogeneity, competition, cooperation, and exploration provide candidate mechanisms; none guarantees progress. Educator agents are important because they can produce learning opportunities and potentially alter the environment of improvement itself. A positive minimal study would prioritize a controlled educator and ecology comparison, followed separately by a weight-level bridge. A negative or mixed result would constrain the mechanism while preserving the record. All proposed empirical outcomes remain unmeasured.
