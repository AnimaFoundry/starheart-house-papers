## Break-even inequalities

| Inequality | Interpretation |
| --- | --- |
| `tau_nu < tau_EM` | Physical chord length is insufficient; the complete request-to-action neutrino path must win. |
| `P_D^stat(m) >= q` and `P_FA^stat(m) <= alpha`, followed by sequence-level `P_D^A` and `P_FA^A` | Statistical and actionable trigger constraints are distinct and joint. |
| `N_nu p_eff >= -ln(1-q)` | Zero-background threshold-one small-`p` particle budget; not a universal reliability result. |
| `rho1[E(Delta G)-C_pulse^A] - rho0 P_FA^A L_FA - C_fixed - C_operate > 0` | Annual private value must use authenticated sequence cost and false-trigger loss relative to fallback. |
| `CAPEX <= PV(operating surplus)` | Finite commercialization horizon bounds recoverable infrastructure cost. |
| `Delta V_h > Delta V_direct` | A relay is justified only by measured flux, detector, availability, or attenuation economics that exceed regeneration penalties. |

## Decisive falsification program

The commercial hypothesis should be rejected, rather than indefinitely deferred, if any of the following survives independent replication over the relevant operating envelope:

1. The lower bound on source actuation, pulse formation, DAQ, classification, and local routing already exceeds the best electromagnetic end-to-end latency, even before waiting for an interaction.
2. A calibrated upper bound on qualifying events per wall-plug joule cannot meet the joint deadline and pulse-cost contour for any viable detector and siting configuration.
3. The upper confidence bound on authenticated false-trigger probability implies greater expected null-window loss than the maximum opportunity gain.
4. Required detector mass, accepted volume, or standoff distance makes the endpoint last mile slower than the propagation advantage.
5. Neither the direct architecture nor any relay configuration has positive end-to-end incremental value once full detection, authentication, regeneration, and infrastructure are counted.
6. The alpha half-life or eligible opportunity duration is shorter than the best achievable authenticated decision latency.
7. Competitive diffusion, a speed bump, frequent batch auction, consolidation, or regulation reduces durable incremental value below operating cost.
8. Independent cost estimates yield negative finite-horizon NPV even under optimistic but evidence-bounded physical performance.

These are observable failure conditions. Replacing a failed system with an unconstrained future source, detector, market, or price assumption does not preserve the tested hypothesis; it defines a new one.

Separately, if every relay configuration has lower end-to-end value than the direct architecture once regeneration is counted, the regenerative-mesh sub-hypothesis is falsified even though a direct-link commercial hypothesis could remain open.
