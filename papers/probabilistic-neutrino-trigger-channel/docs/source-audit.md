## Audit rules for additional sources

Load-bearing quantitative rows used by the manuscript or target sheet are mirrored in [`../results/parameter_provenance.csv`](../results/parameter_provenance.csv). The narrative audit also records qualitative design evidence that is not promoted to a numerical parameter. For disagreements, both values and scopes are retained. Quantities without a directly inspected locator remain `LITERATURE GAP` and are not used as measured scenario inputs.

The manuscript distinguishes:

- exact physical constants from measured system parameters;
- event timestamp resolution from end-to-end decision latency;
- reported facility performance from the proposed architecture;
- propagation-only calculations from route and processing measurements;
- peer-reviewed market estimates from synthetic utility scenarios.
