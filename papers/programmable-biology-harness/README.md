# A Verifiable Research Harness for Programmable Biological Systems

Anonymous English conceptual preprint, version 0.1.0, 14 November 2026.

[Read the complete paper](manuscript.md) | [Prospective study specification](protocol.json) | [Agent entry](llms.txt)

Research question: Can explicit validation allocation and separation of research-policy revision from confirmation improve useful discoveries per total cost over tuned active-learning baselines?

Scope: Abstract synthetic causal systems only. The paper includes analytical arguments and a prospective evaluation design. No empirical experiments, training, human studies, biological interventions, or device demonstrations have been performed for this preprint. It is not peer reviewed.

The study specification is incomplete for execution: sample size, numerical acceptance thresholds, seeds, and the confirmation analysis must be frozen in a new protocol before any study. The specification grants no execution authority or resource budget.
